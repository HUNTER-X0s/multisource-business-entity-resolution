## 1. Experimental Rationale & Setup

### 1.1 The Theoretical Hypothesis
In multi-source business entity resolution, the vast majority of candidate negatives generated by blocking channels are "lexically near" or share high token overlap with true entities (e.g. same business name, different branch). 

Standard binary cross-entropy treats all negatives uniformly. The hypothesis of EXP-09 was:
- Identify "hard negatives" that share identical business names or high name similarity ($>0.85$) but represent different real-world businesses.
- Apply a $2\times$ sample weight penalty to hard negatives during LightGBM training:
$$w_i = \begin{cases} 2.0 & \text{if pair is a Hard Negative} \\ 1.0 & \text{if pair is a Standard Negative or Positive} \end{cases}$$
- **Expected Outcome:** Suppress false positives on same-name branch collisions, increasing Precision and Macro $F_{0.5}$.

## 3. Root Cause Autopsy: The Singleton Collapse Mechanism

Why did upweighting hard negatives destroy performance?

### 1. Probability Calibration Distortion
By doubling the loss gradient on 40% of the negative training pairs, the empirical prior $P(y=1)$ was artificially skewed downward. The raw output probabilities no longer represented the true posterior match probabilities.

### 2. Threshold Shift and the "No-Match" Cliff
Because all candidate probabilities were artificially suppressed towards zero, the optimal global threshold shifted from $\theta = 0.55$ up to $\theta = 0.80$.

At $\theta = 0.80$:
- The decision boundary became hypersensitive to small calibration noise.
- On queries with **zero true matches (true singletons)**, slight probability elevations on candidate noise crossed the threshold, causing **Singleton Accuracy to collapse from 91.33% down to 75.75%**.
- In the competition Macro $F_{0.5}$ metric, a false positive on a true singleton yields $F_{0.5} = 0.0$ (the singleton cliff). A 15.6% drop in singleton accuracy causes an immediate, catastrophic penalty to the global query-average score.

## 4. Architectural Policy & Guidelines

1. **REJECT Uniform Loss Reweighting:**
   - Artificially reweighting loss instances distorts probability calibration without providing discriminative signals to tree split points.
2. **Precision Must Be Enforced Structurally:**
   - Rather than penalizing negatives globally via loss weights, false positive suppression must be achieved via **explicit negative contradiction features** (e.g., `contradiction_house_no`, `contradiction_postal`) and **calibrated query-level decision rules**.
3. **Singleton Guardrail:**
   - Any proposed training modification that reduces Singleton Accuracy below 90% is rejected unconditionally.
