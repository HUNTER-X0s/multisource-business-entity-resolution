## 1. Operating Mandate & Audit Summary

This compliance matrix documents the empirical audit of the Amazon ML Challenge 2026 dataset (12.53M training rows, 11.70M test rows across Sources 1, 2, and 3). 

Every technique in the Phase 1A audit framework was evaluated against the **Central Rule**:
> *Inspect → Hypothesize → Research → Measure → Experiment → Compare → Decide → Implement only when justified.*

Techniques are classified into precise, evidence-grounded states:
- **`IMPLEMENTED`**: Production-safe, implemented, tested, and active.
- **`PARTIALLY IMPLEMENTED`**: Functional core active; secondary extensions documented.
- **`NOT APPLICABLE`**: Irrelevant to the Business Entity Resolution objective or dataset structure.
- **`HARMFUL`**: Demonstrated to degrade recall, cause catastrophic collisions, or hallucinate data.
- **`REJECTED`**: Empirically measured and discarded due to ambiguity or poor performance.
- **`DEFERRED`**: Postponed to later phases (e.g., Phase 2 feature engineering or Phase 3 classifier).
- **`EXPERIMENTAL`**: Evaluated in controlled benchmarks; reserved as candidate fallback channels.
